## 1. Project one-pager

### What it is
Arbor Sentinel is an open-source civic-technology platform that
organizes verified public evidence, tracks legal and political
responsibility, and helps people take lawful action — starting with Gaza and
the wider regional crisis. The structure is modular so it can support other
documented mass-atrocity contexts later.

### What it is not
- Not a court, an official authority, or a legal adviser.
- Not a campaign against any identity group.
- Not a funding intermediary or a charity.
- Not a platform for witness submissions (during the static beta).

### Why it exists
To give citizens, journalists, researchers, and policymakers a calm,
sourced, lawful way to navigate evidence, legal developments, humanitarian
information, and action pathways around atrocity accountability — with
corrections built into the trust model.

### Status
Public static beta. Content is reviewed and labeled honestly (source
quality vs. editorial status). Corrections and bug reporting are open.

### The evidence model
Every record carries a source reference and a review status. The platform
separates sources, leads, allegations, legal findings, and reviewed public
evidence (see `docs/methodology.md`).

## 5. Quote policy

- **The only authorized quotable source is the spokesperson.** All quotes
  must be attributed to the spokesperson by name/title, or to the project
  ("Arbor Sentinel said…").
- No contributor, volunteer, or listed organization may be quoted on behalf
  of the project.
- We do not provide quotes that claim partnerships, endorsements, or
  positions on behalf of listed organizations.
- Interview requests go through the press contact. The spokesperson
  approves all interviews.
- If an error appears in a story, we will issue a correction via the
  spokesperson; we do not retract the underlying public record without a
  documented correction.

---

## 6. What we will not claim

To keep the record credible, the press kit explicitly excludes:

- Any partnership, endorsement, or funding relationship unless confirmed in
  writing and documented in `docs/partnership-policy.md`.
- That the platform is a court, official authority, or legal adviser.
- That listed organizations endorse the project.
- Coverage, user, or traffic figures not in §2.
- That the project represents or speaks for victims or affected communities.
