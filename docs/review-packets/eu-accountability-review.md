### Non-trivial judgment calls:

1. **"Indications of breach" vs. "material breach":** The High Representative's June 2025 review found "indications" Israel was in breach of Article 2. The Commission's September 2025 proposal cites a "material breach" as the legal basis for suspension. The review packet records both formulations — the findings review was at the "indications" level; the legal basis for suspension requires a "material breach" determination under the Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties. Legal reviewer should confirm this distinction is correctly captured.

2. **"Man-made famine" characterization:** The EP resolution describes an "IPC-confirmed man-made famine in northern Gaza." The IPC's own technical classification uses Phase 5 (Famine). The EP's "man-made" characterization is political, not IPC terminology. The review packet records the EP's characterization as attributed to the EP — the platform does not independently characterize the causes.

3. **Competency "Shared" label:** EU humanitarian aid is labelled "Shared" in competency records. Under EU law, humanitarian aid is a shared competence, but in practice DG ECHO operates with significant autonomy. The competency descriptions note that member states retain independent bilateral programmes alongside EU action.
