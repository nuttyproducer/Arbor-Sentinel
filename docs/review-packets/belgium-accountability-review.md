## Legal Terminology Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| "Belgium adopted sanctions" not "Belgium imposed sanctions" — sanctions are applied, not imposed | Precision: Belgium applies restrictive measures; "imposed" carries a connotation of unilateral imposition |
| "Persona non grata declaration" — a diplomatic status, not a criminal finding | Legal accuracy: persona non grata is a diplomatic designation under the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations |
| Court ruling described as "judicial order — not a policy enacted by the executive or legislature" | Separation of powers: the court's order against Flanders is a judicial remedy, not a policy choice by elected government |
| "De facto arms embargo" attributed to campaigners, not stated as platform assessment | Attribution: the platform reports what campaigners describe; it does not independently characterize the regulatory situation |
| "The resolution is a political statement — it is not legally binding" (for EP and Brussels parliament resolutions) | Procedural accuracy: parliamentary resolutions express political will; they do not create legal obligations unless adopted as legislation |
